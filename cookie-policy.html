Cookie Policy

How this website uses browser storage and optional analytics.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Essential preference storage

The website stores your cookie-banner choice in your browser so it can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This preference is used to provide the consent functionality you requested.

Google Analytics

When enabled by the website owner and accepted by the visitor, Google Analytics may set cookies or use similar technology to collect information about website use. It is not loaded before analytics consent is granted in this implementation.

Your choice

You can accept or reject analytics from the website banner. To change the choice later, use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer, or clear this website’s stored data in your browser.

Update this policy

Review this policy whenever you add advertising, embedded video, live chat, maps, payment tools or another service that may use cookies or similar technologies.
