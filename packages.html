Frequently asked questions

Before you order

Why are prices confirmed after a survey?

Property size, construction, cable routes, system compatibility and equipment quantities affect the final scope. A survey prevents unsuitable equipment and hidden installation assumptions.

Can I buy equipment without installation?

Some equipment may be supplied separately, but managed networks, access systems and automation normally require professional configuration. Ask us about the specific product you need.

Do you cover my area?

SREX serves projects across the UK. Remote discovery and support may be available before an on-site visit.

Can a package be expanded later?

Yes. Solutions are designed with future capacity in mind, subject to the selected platform and installed infrastructure.
