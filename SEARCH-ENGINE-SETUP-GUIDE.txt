SREX SEARCH ENGINE, BUSINESS AND COMMERCE SETUP
================================================

CANONICAL WEBSITE
-----------------
https://srexsolutions.com/

Before submission, ensure www.srexsolutions.com has DNS and redirects with a
301 response to the non-www address. The SSL certificate should cover both.

GOOGLE
------
1. Search Console: add the Domain property srexsolutions.com and verify it by
   adding Google's TXT record in DNS.
2. Submit https://srexsolutions.com/sitemap.xml in Search Console.
3. Inspect the homepage, packages page and service pages; request indexing
   after the deployed URL passes Live Test.
4. GA4 is configured with Measurement ID G-TKSLVRE08K. Test only after
   accepting analytics cookies, then check Realtime and DebugView.
5. Claim or create the Google Business Profile. Use exactly:
   Business name: SREX Solutions
   Website: https://srexsolutions.com/
   Phone: 07417 580 544
   Email: info@srexsolutions.com
   Service area: only the areas the business genuinely serves.
6. Add opening hours, real project photographs, services and accurate business
   categories. Do not publish a residential address unless customers visit it.

BING, YAHOO, DUCKDUCKGO AND ECOSIA
---------------------------------
1. Add the site in Bing Webmaster Tools. Importing the verified Google Search
   Console property is the quickest supported route.
2. Submit https://srexsolutions.com/sitemap.xml.
3. Review Site Scan and SEO reports after Bing has crawled the site.
4. Bing discovery also helps search experiences that use Bing's index. Yahoo,
   DuckDuckGo and Ecosia do not require separate changes to these HTML pages.

INDEXNOW
--------
IndexNow can notify participating engines when pages are added or materially
changed. Generate a key in Bing Webmaster Tools or IndexNow, place the matching
key text file at the website root, and submit changed canonical URLs. Do not
invent a key or publish a key that has not been registered for this host.

OTHER SEARCH ENGINES
--------------------
For Yandex Webmaster, Naver Search Advisor or another market-specific console,
verify site ownership and submit the same canonical sitemap. Only create these
accounts when the target market is relevant to the business.

PACKAGES, PRODUCTS AND PAYMENTS
-------------------------------
packages.html is an indexable service-package catalogue with request links.
Prices are intentionally not marked up because installed systems require a
survey and no approved prices, VAT treatment or payment account were supplied.

Before enabling immediate online purchase, provide and approve:
- exact package and optional-extra prices;
- whether prices include VAT;
- geographical availability and installation lead times;
- cancellation, refund, warranty and service terms;
- a Stripe, PayPal or other checkout account;
- product identifiers, availability and images for physical goods;
- shipping and returns terms when equipment is sold for delivery.

After those details exist, add secure hosted checkout links and eligible
Product/Offer structured data. Use Google Merchant Center only for genuine
products or eligible offers that meet its policies; do not submit quotation-only
services as fixed-price retail products.

DEPLOYMENT CHECKLIST
--------------------
- Upload all HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
- Confirm every canonical URL returns HTTP 200.
- Confirm HTTP and www redirect once to https://srexsolutions.com/.
- Confirm robots.txt and sitemap.xml are publicly accessible.
- Test structured data with Google Rich Results Test.
- Test mobile layout, enquiry links and package preselection.
- Accept analytics in a private browser and confirm a GA4 Realtime visit.
- Submit the sitemap to Google and Bing after deployment.
